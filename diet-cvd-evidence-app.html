Artificial and non-sugar sweeteners

Non-sugar sweeteners activate sweet-taste receptors (T1R2/T1R3) with little or no glycaemic carbohydrate contribution. They are not essential nutrients and should not replace a balanced dietary pattern.

Clinical recommendations
  • Prefer unsweetened drinks and gradual reduction in overall sweetness.
  • WHO 2023 conditionally advises against using non-sugar sweeteners for long-term weight control or NCD prevention; people with pre-existing diabetes are outside this recommendation's scope.
  • For diabetes, replacing sugar may reduce immediate carbohydrate intake, but assess the whole food, glucose response and nutritional quality.
  • Check FSSAI label requirements, especially phenylketonuria warnings for aspartame and child-related warnings.
  • Pregnancy, children and complex chronic disease require individualised dietary advice.
